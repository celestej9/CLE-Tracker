How to File in August 2026 — OCA Step-by-Step

New York does not use a paper form for standard registration. You file entirely online through the OCA portal. Follow these steps when your deadline approaches.

1
Gather your documents before you start
Have this tracker's printed summary, your credit card (registration fee ~$375 for most attorneys), and your NY registration number ready.
2
Log into OCA Attorney Online Services
Go to iapps.courts.state.ny.us/attorney/AttorneyHome. Create an account if you don't have one — you'll need your registration number and the last 4 digits of your SSN.
3
Click "Attorney Registration" and select your biennial period
Select the upcoming two-year period. Note: you register for the upcoming period but report CLE for the prior period. The portal will prompt you for both.
4
Complete the CLE certification section
Enter your total credit hours completed. You do not list individual courses — you only certify the total and that you met the mandatory category minimums. Use this tracker's summary for your numbers.
5
Certify and pay
You'll certify under penalty of perjury that you've complied. Then pay the registration fee. Keep your certificates on file for 4 years in case of audit — you do not submit them to OCA.
6
Save your confirmation
Print or save the confirmation page. OCA will also email a confirmation to your address on file. Keep this with your CLE certificates.
⚠ The CLE Update Form you have is only needed if OCA grants you an extension, modification, or waiver — or to correct a prior filing error. It is not used for standard registration. File it at iapps.courts.state.ny.us/courtforms/cle/ only if instructed by the CLE Board.
